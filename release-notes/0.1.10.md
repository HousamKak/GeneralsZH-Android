## en
# Sign in to GeneralsOnline from the Online button
- Multiplayer, Online asks you to sign in when you are not, opens the sign-in and brings you back to the game
- The ZH COMMANDER screen is organised in Game, Online and Support sections
- The Online section shows who you are signed in as, with Sign in and Sign out
- More settings is now Advanced: drivers, diagnostics and language packs
- Crash reports now point to the exact place in the code, so fixes come faster
