What you get

  • The real engine

    The 2003 game code compiled for ARM phones. No emulator in between.

  • Built for touch

    Tap to select, drag to box-select, two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom.

  • 30 or 60 Hz

    Retail game speed, or 60 Hz to match the GeneralsOnline PC client.

  • Online and LAN

    GeneralsOnline lobbies, quickmatch and PC cross-play, plus LAN games between phones on the same Wi-Fi.

  • Two renderers

    OpenGL ES by default, Vulkan where your phone handles it.

  • 13 languages, Arabic included

    Game text packs, with right-to-left layout for Arabic.
