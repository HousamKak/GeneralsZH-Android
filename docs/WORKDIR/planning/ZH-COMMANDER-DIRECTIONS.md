# ZH Commander: directions and recommendations

Written 10/10/2026, launch day (0.1.20 published). Where the project can go next, and in what order.

## Where things stand

- ZH Commander 0.1.20 is live: activation, in-app updates with release notes (English and
  Arabic), game data packs with optional mod packs, online play through GeneralsOnline with PC
  cross-play, a support report that reaches the developer.
- App Monitor tracks crashes, installs, the website funnel (visits, downloads, buy clicks),
  returning players, and from 0.1.20 matches, frame rate by phone, settings and online problems.
- Keys sell through Whish only, which works in Lebanon only. Card and PayPal checkout exists but
  is not switched on.
- Free alternatives exist on GitHub (the MYSOREZ port this project started from, tarek369's
  port). Players there bring their own game files. What ZH Commander sells is the one-tap game
  data download, the updates and fixes, and support.

## 1. Make the game itself better

Keeps buyers happy and earns word of mouth.

- **Performance on weak phones.** Frame rate by phone model, renderer and game speed now arrives
  with every match. Fix the phones that struggle, worst first, instead of guessing.
- **Mods.** The optional pack system is already built (MODS screen, gameplay-pack online guard).
  The big Zero Hour mods (Contra, ShockWave, Rise of the Reds) keep the PC community alive.
  "Contra on your phone" is a launch on its own. Needs each mod team's permission; offer them as
  free packs.
- **Controllers.** Gamepad, and mouse and keyboard. Opens Android gaming handhelds (AYN Odin,
  Retroid), tablets and ChromeOS: enthusiasts who pay for exactly this kind of game.
- **Campaign and challenges** where anything is rough, and **cloud saves** through the existing
  server.

## 2. Build community around phone multiplayer

- Phone-to-phone online play is the hook: the strongest reason for a group of friends to buy.
- An own Discord server: announcements, bug reports, match-up channels, weekend tournaments.
- A simple leaderboard on the site.
- A closer relationship with GeneralsOnline (a phone section, or being listed as a client) puts
  the app in front of every active player.

## 3. Make the business sturdier

- **Payments:** turn on card and PayPal, so keys sell outside Lebanon. Consider regional pricing.
- **Free demo:** one skirmish map without a key. More downloads, and buyers who already know the
  game runs on their phone.
- **A 3-key pack** for friends who want to play online together.
- **Legal footing.** The risk is EA's game data, not the code: the engine is GPL, so selling
  builds is fine while the source stays available (keep the source link on the site). Offer both
  models: "bring your own files" (the safest, and how the free ports work) and the convenient
  one-tap download. If EA objects, switch off the download and keep the business.
- **Distribution:** Google Play will not accept it (EA's trademark). The site, and possibly
  itch.io, which hosts this kind of fan project.

## 4. Reuse what was built

The biggest long-term option. In two days the project gained a complete independent
distribution stack, none of it specific to Zero Hour: licensing and activation, signed updates,
game data packs, telemetry and crash decoding, support reports, an admin console, the site,
release notes in two languages.

- **A second game.** EA has also released source code under GPL for C&C Tiberian Dawn, Red
  Alert and Renegade. Red Alert for Android on the same stack is the natural second title. The
  same EA data question applies, so plan "bring your own files" from day one.
- **Arabic-first classic games.** Proper Arabic support for classic games is a niche nobody
  serves well, and this project already does it.
- **The platform itself.** App Monitor plus the licensing and update stack could serve other
  indie developers who distribute outside the app stores.

## Marketing, short term

- Go where Zero Hour players already are, in order of payoff: Arabic Generals communities
  (large Facebook groups; Arabic support is the unique angle), Reddit (r/commandandconquer,
  r/AndroidGaming, r/EmulationOnAndroid, with an honest story post), C&C Discords and
  GameReplays (ask moderators first), and C&C YouTubers and streamers with free keys.
- Short videos over posters: 15 to 30 second clips of a phone in hand, a new one every day or two.
- A different link per channel (for example `zerohour.housamkak.com/?src=ig`), counted in App
  Monitor, to see which channel brings buyers.
- The louder the launch, the more likely EA notices the paid data download. Keep the EA
  disclaimer everywhere and the "use your own files" fallback working.

## Recommended order

1. **Next 2 weeks:** card and PayPal checkout, a Discord server, then fix the worst phones the
   new statistics reveal. Making the paid product solid comes first.
2. **Next month:** controller support, and one major mod (start by asking the Contra team). Each
   is a second launch moment with something new to show.
3. **After that:** decide whether this stays one great product or becomes a small studio of
   classic-game ports. The infrastructure for the second option already exists.
